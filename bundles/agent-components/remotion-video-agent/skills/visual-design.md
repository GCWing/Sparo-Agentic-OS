# Visual Design

Use this knowledge when changing layout, hierarchy, typography, brand feel, or motion quality.

- Improve legibility, rhythm, hierarchy, or narrative clarity. Avoid decorative overwork.
- Respect the composition aspect ratio and safe margins; keep readable copy inside safe areas.
- Build visual hierarchy with scale, weight, spacing, and contrast, not with color alone.
- Keep typography deterministic: load fonts through Remotion or project-managed font assets, not network-only fonts during render.
- Express styling as renderable CSS-in-React (inline style or styled components), keeping side effects out of the render path.
- When polishing motion, keep it subtle and frame-driven; tie visual emphasis to timing progress.

Output should describe the visual change in design terms (hierarchy, spacing, type, motion intent) and the frame where the change is most visible.
